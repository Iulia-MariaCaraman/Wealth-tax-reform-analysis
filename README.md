# Wealth Tax Reform Analysis: The Zucman Tax Debate in France

Academic analytical essay prepared for the Faculty of Cybernetics, Statistics and Economic Informatics at ASE Bucharest.

## Overview
This paper examines the political and economic controversy surrounding the proposed wealth tax on ultra-high-net-worth individuals in France, widely referred to as the Zucman Tax. 

The study reviews an analysis of Frances fiscal dynamics, exploring the paradox between the countrys luxury goods industry and the public sentiment toward extreme wealth accumulation.

## Key Themes and Arguments
* The Fiscal Dilemma: Frances overall tax burden is among the highest in Europe, yet high-wealth households often face lower effective tax rates through aggressive optimization and specialized structures.
* Arguments in Favor: Economists such as Thomas Piketty, Joseph Stiglitz, and Paul Krugman argue that a targeted minimum wealth tax restores civic fairness, curbs expanding wealth disparities, and closes avoidance loopholes.
* Arguments Against: Business representatives, government officials, and critics caution that aggressive wealth taxation risks capital flight, reduces investment incentives, and damages international investor confidence.
* The Political Balance: Navigating legislative budget negotiations while attempting to preserve Frances competitiveness as an enterprise-friendly economy.
